1 Introduction

Welcome to EduPortal. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

2 Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you register.

3 How We Use Your Information

  • To provide educational notes, tests, and learning resources.

4 WhatsApp Communication

We use the WhatsApp Business API (Meta Platforms) solely to send you educational content, notes links, or updates that you request. Your phone number is never used for spam or sold to anyone.

📱 You may opt out at any time by messaging "STOP" on WhatsApp or contacting us via email.

5 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with trusted service providers required to operate our platform (e.g., Firebase for secure storage, Meta for WhatsApp delivery).

6 Data Security

Your passwords are encrypted using industry-standard hashing (Bcrypt), and all data is stored securely on protected servers. We continuously work to keep your information safe.

7 Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
